Mobility & Education Drive

Cycle Project - Hope for Education

Make life a little Easier… Donate a Bi-cycle (New / Refurbished)

In rural areas of India, more than 60% of children drop out of school after 5th grade. High schools are not available in rural areas and students need to travel long distances to pursue higher education. Long walks, unavailability of public transportation, and safety are huge concerns for girl students and the main reason for dropping out of schools in India.

Family financial constraints and the non-affordability of bicycles (which could be a safe mode of transport) are the main reasons why 32.5% of girls drop out at secondary levels.

To address the commuting challenges faced by the underprivileged, SAHAS aims to give opportunity to students by providing bicycles and fulfilling their dream of going to school. The journey to school will become safer, faster than before, and life would be a little easier for them.

An Appeal

Many societies have discarded bicycles which they sell as scrap. Such cycles, refurbished, could be donated to these needy children in remote villages of Maharashtra.

SAHAS would be thankful to the donors and can also arrange for the transportation of such bicycles to the needy children.

The cost of a new bicycle is anywhere between Rs 4,000 to 5,000. Even second-hand bicycles cost around Rs 2,000. Added to that is the transportation cost. Even a small amount of Rs 500/- from you would advance us towards our goal of making owning a cycle a reality for children in the villages of Maharashtra.
