Supporting TB Patients for a TB-Mukt Bharat

Tuberculosis (TB) is a global health threat that can pose a significant risk to a country. This was brought home to us while discussing healthcare with some doctors. Poor nutrition of the marginalised makes them prone to this killing disease. Though medical treatment for the patient is available free of cost, nutrition is of utmost importance — not only for the one affected but for all family members.

This year the Government is propagating the cause of TB-Mukt Bharat by holding awareness programmes. More than 40,000 people are affected in Mumbai alone.

Becoming a Ni-kshay Mitra and providing food under the Nikshay Poshan Yojana launched by the Government is one way of providing critical support. The Ni-kshay Mitra Food Basket Scheme ensures that patients receive a nutritional diet as recommended by the World Health Organization (WHO). Per patient, the Government share is Rs 1,000/-. The balance requirement is supplemented by individuals, NGOs, and corporate entities.

Our Impact & Adoption Drive

SAHAS is a self-financed organisation. We at SAHAS [Spreading Aid Health and Smiles] Foundation thought it a good enough cause. Last year, we adopted 10 TB patients under the Nikshay Poshan Scheme, and this year, with the help of friends and well-wishers, we have adopted about 22 patients.

With your help, we can reach out to many more in need.
