July 02, 2026 – Madhuban Farms

sahasfoundationmum
Jul 21, 2026 · 1 min read

SAHAS held a health awareness camp for tribal children of Katkari and Thakar communities at Madhuban Agro Farms, Pimpalwadi, Dhodhani, Panvel. To make them aware of the importance of daily healthy habits, every attending woman was provided with a healthcare and hygiene kit consisting of essential items like toothbrush, toothpaste, and sanitary pads. The kit also contained calcium and vitamin supplements with clear written instructions in Marathi.
