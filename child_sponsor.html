Child Sponsorship

Child Sponsorship for a Happy Childhood

Child sponsorship is a program that enables you to support children, their families, and entire communities — giving them the chance to grow up healthy, educated, and safe – forever changing their life and future. It is to give children a healthy start in life, the opportunity to learn, and protection from harm.

Child sponsorship is one of the best methods for helping families living in extreme poverty rise out of poverty. It’s also a great activity for kids to participate in to learn important life lessons.

All money received from sponsors and donors are pooled to make a powerful investment in children’s lives and futures! The donated funds are often not spent specifically on the sponsored child, but pooled with other contributions to fund a variety of education, health, security, infrastructure, or other projects in the child's community.

The Five Key Pillars:

Education
Clean Water & Sanitation
Food & Nutrition
Healthcare
Income & Empowerment

According to a poll of top development economists, child sponsorship is the most effective long-term development intervention for fighting poverty and helping the poor. It not only addresses a child's immediate physical needs, improving children's lives, it also builds self-esteem and self-respect in the marginalized.

Children are the future of humanity. They are our hope. Our aspiration. But, not all children are lucky enough to have even the basic necessities. Lakhs of children roam the streets without home or family. There are about 1.77 million homeless of which about 5 Lakh to 7 Lakh are street children. Further, the true figure became larger in the post-COVID pandemic as many more people from the country's lowest quarter got impacted. We need your help to bring down the numbers.

  • Infrastructure Need: By funding the purchase of bicycles for remote transportation
  • School Essentials: Uniforms, books, bags, water bottles, and learning supplies

Note: If the particular need is already fulfilled by the time you gift, your contribution will be directed to other similar needs and you will be informed of the same.
